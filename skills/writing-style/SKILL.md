# Writing style

An example skill. Edit it to match how you want documents to read.

## Rules

1. **Summary first.** Open with 3 to 5 plain lines: the outcome and what it means for the reader.
2. **Every word earns its place.** Cut restated context, hedging and discarded options.
3. **Dot points over paragraphs.** One idea per line. Headings are fine.
4. **Written for its reader.** Name the audience, then drop what they do not need. Explain jargon.
5. **Proportionate length.** If it runs past a few pages, distil it or move detail into an appendix.
6. **Dates at the end of file names:** `plan-2026-10-14.md`.

## Check before delivering

- Can the reader get the point from the first five lines?
- Is anything there only because it was easy to include?
